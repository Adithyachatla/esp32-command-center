# Code of Conduct

This is a small, friendly learning project. Everyone is welcome — students, makers and experienced engineers alike.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and respectful. Beginners will ask basic questions; answer them without condescension.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Assume good intent.
- Keep discussions on topic: the project, ESP32 development and related learning.
- Protect privacy: never post other people's personal information, and never share your own credentials or network details.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, insults, threats or personal attacks of any kind
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Spam, advertising unrelated to the project, or deliberately disruptive behaviour

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues or pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.
Report problems privately to the maintainer through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (do **not** post them publicly).
Reports are handled in good faith and with discretion.

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions). It is a plain-language policy written for this
project, not a legal document.
